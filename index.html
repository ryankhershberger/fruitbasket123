Fruit Basket 123

A gentle counting app for kids ages 3–6

Children learn to count by playing with fruit: counting it, picking it, comparing it, blending smoothies, serving animal friends at a fruit stand, and feeding a hungry monkey. A calm narrator speaks every instruction, so no reading is needed. Mistakes lead to friendly hints, never a "wrong" sound.

For parents

  • No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts, and no tracking.
  • Works completely offline. Nothing is ever sent from the device.
  • The parent area (the small lock on the Home screen) is behind a grown-up question. It shows progress for each game and has settings: Spanish counting, sound effects, the highest level, and reset.
  • Games adjust their difficulty on their own (numbers 1–5, 1–10, then 1–20).

Common questions

The narrator is quiet. Turn up the device volume. Narration plays even when the silent switch is on. Sound effects can be turned off in the parent area.

How do I reset progress? Tap the lock on the Home screen, answer the grown-up question, then choose Reset progress. Settings are kept.

Is my child's progress backed up? Progress is saved only on this device. Deleting the app deletes the progress.
